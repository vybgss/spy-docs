Privacy Policy

Spy: Guess the Place

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version

Spy: Guess the Place collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and no servers of ours. The game is played offline, on one phone, passed around the table — and it stays that way.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no profiles, and no tracking of any kind. It never asks for your name, your contacts, your location, or your photos, and it has no code in it that would send anything anywhere.

2. What stays on your phone

So the game remembers your table between evenings, a few settings are saved locally on the device:

  • the number of players and spies;
  • which location packs are selected;
  • the chosen language, and the sound and vibration switches;
  • the last few locations drawn, so the same place does not come up twice in a row;
  • how many rounds have been played to the end, so the app knows when to ask for an App Store rating.

This is ordinary app storage on your own device. It is never transmitted, it is not synced to iCloud or anywhere else, and deleting the app removes it completely. Rounds themselves are not saved at all: who was the spy and where you were is gone the moment the round ends.

3. Internet

The game works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. The only time your phone talks to a server is when you buy or restore Premium — that connection is to Apple's App Store, not to us.

4. Purchases

The Premium unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any of your devices.

5. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
