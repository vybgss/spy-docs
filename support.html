Support

Everyone at the table shares one location — except the spy.
Answers to common questions are below.

How is it played?

One phone goes round the table. Each player looks at their card in private: everyone sees the same location, and the spy sees only the word SPY. Then you question each other in a circle — the table tries to expose the spy, the spy tries to work out where everybody is. The app deals the cards; the arguing is up to you.

Do we need anything besides the phone?

No. One device, passed around, no internet, no accounts, nothing to set up. It works on a train, in a cabin, or on a plane in airplane mode.

How many players and spies?

From 3 players — up to 4 free, and up to 50 with Premium. The number of spies is capped so at least two players always know the location; otherwise there would be nobody left to question.

What is free and what does Premium unlock?

The Basic pack and tables of up to 4 players are free, with no time limit and no ads. The one-time Premium purchase unlocks every other location pack — including packs added in future updates — and raises the table to 50 players.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any device signed in with it.

Can the same location come up twice in a row?

No. A location that has just been used is held back for the next nine rounds, so ten games in a row always give the table ten different places.

Can I see the locations in a pack before playing?

Yes. Tap the list icon on the right of any pack to read every location it contains — useful for checking that all the words will be familiar to your table.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The game is available in English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese, and Portuguese, locations included. By default it follows your phone's language (if supported); you can change it any time in Settings.

Can I turn off the card sound or the vibration?

Both are in Settings and can be switched off independently. Handy when you are playing somewhere quiet — or when someone is trying to guess the spy by the sound of the flip.

Do my settings sync between devices?

No. Players, spies, packs and language are stored on each device separately, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Only the Premium purchase follows your Apple Account.

System requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No internet connection required.
